# Verdan Brand System

## Identity

| Element | Value |
|---------|-------|
| **Name** | Verdan |
| **Tagline** | Confidence backed by modern care. |
| **Positioning** | Discreet, physician-guided ED treatment for men who value privacy, quality, and modern care. |

## Color Palette

| Token | Hex | Role |
|-------|-----|------|
| Forest | `#1A3D30` | Primary — trust, discretion |
| Forest Deep | `#0C1F17` | Depth, cinematic sections |
| Ivory | `#F8F5EF` | Warm backgrounds |
| Sage | `#8DA698` | Secondary UI |
| Copper | `#C07855` | CTA & accent |

**Reasoning:** Forest and ivory signal calm, premium lifestyle-health — distinct from Keeps (navy/yellow) and MEDVi (bright blue). Copper adds human warmth at conversion without alarm red or clinical blue.
